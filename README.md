# piracy-climate

**Do sea-surface temperature shocks drive maritime piracy, and does the effect still hold in recent years?**

*Status: work in progress. Day 1 of a first version.*

## Data feasibility (26 Oct 2026)

- **Source:** NGA Anti-Shipping Activity Messages (ASAM), shapefile downloaded on 26 Oct 2026 from msi.nga.mil.
- **Coverage:** 9,182 incidents, May 1978 – June 2024. The database is no longer updated (last entry: 1 July 2024).
- **Study zones:** 5,155 geolocated incidents since 2000 in the four zones (Gulf of Aden/Somali basin, Gulf of Guinea, Strait of Malacca, South China Sea); no missing coordinates.
- **Caveats:**
  - Houthi missile and drone attacks dominate the Aden zone in 2023-24; they are not piracy and will be filtered out.
  - Possible reporting break around 2018-2020 (incidents halve, then double), to be checked against IMB figures.
  - Zone boundaries will be revised: the Aden box cuts off high-seas Somali attacks, and the Indonesian archipelago is missing.
